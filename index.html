DS Fleet Mobility focuses on safe, comfortable and dependable passenger transport. We offer dashcam-equipped vehicles, quality 5-star-rated car options and selected electric vehicles, depending on availability, routes and travel requirements.

01

Safety with Dashcams

Selected vehicles are equipped with dashcams to support journey monitoring and promote greater awareness of road conditions. Dashcam availability depends on the vehicle assigned to your journey.

02

Quality 5-Star Rated Cars

Where available, we can offer vehicles with 5-star safety ratings to help customers make informed choices. Ratings depend on the specific model and its applicable safety assessment.

03

Electric Vehicles Also Available

Selected electric vehicles are available alongside conventional vehicles. We consider the route, passenger requirements, vehicle availability and journey needs before confirming a suitable option.
